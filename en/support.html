Support

How do I start?

Play the three tutorials from the home screen. Drag pipes onto the board and connect their openings to the pond.

How is progress saved?

Unlocks, best levels, audio settings and the starting state of an unfinished round are stored on your device. Resume restores the round from its starting state.

How do I contact the developer?

Include your device model, OS version, app version and steps to reproduce the issue. Remove any personal information from screenshots first.

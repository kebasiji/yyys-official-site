Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Game data

The game has no accounts, ads, analytics tracking or cloud saves. Level progress, best levels, audio settings and round snapshots are stored on your device for use by the game.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains in the app’s storage. Use Delete App in your device settings to remove the game and its local data from that device.

Support email

If you choose to email us, we use your email address and the information you include to handle your request. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the issue. Use the address below to ask about this data or request deletion.

Website

This static site has no accounts, forms, ads or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and basic request logs to serve the site, maintain security and troubleshoot issues. Opening this page from the game does not send saves, scores or other game data.
